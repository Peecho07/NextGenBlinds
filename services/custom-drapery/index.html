Common Questions

Custom Drapery FAQ

Can I mix drapery with blinds or shades?

Absolutely — layering drapery over shades or shutters is one of our most requested looks, for both style and extra light control.

How long does custom drapery take to arrive?

Lead times vary by fabric and workroom, typically a few weeks after your in-home measure. We'll give you a firm timeline at your consultation.

Do you install the hardware too?

Yes — our team installs and levels all rods, tracks, and brackets as part of the job.
