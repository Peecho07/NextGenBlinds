Common Questions

Plantation Shutters FAQ

How long does installation take?

Most homes are measured in one visit and installed in a single day once your custom shutters arrive from the manufacturer.

Are plantation shutters worth the investment?

Yes — unlike blinds, shutters are a permanent fixture that hold up for decades and are frequently cited as a value-add by real estate appraisers.

Can shutters handle Florida humidity?

Our composite and PVC shutter options are built specifically to resist warping, fading, and moisture damage.
