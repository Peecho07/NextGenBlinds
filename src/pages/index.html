Motorization

Shades that move on your schedule

Motorized shades can open with the morning, close against afternoon heat, or lower together with one button. They're especially useful on tall windows and wide sliding doors.

  • Control by handheld remote, wall switch, app, or schedule, depending on the system
  • Battery and wired power options, which affect installation
  • Voice and smart-home control need a compatible motor and hub

Layering

One look by day, another by night

Pairing a shade with drapery lets you control light and privacy separately. Keep the view during the day, then close the drapery for a softer, more private room in the evening.

Questions

Good to know before you choose

What does custom window treatment pricing depend on?

Mainly the number and size of openings, the product and fabric or material, how it operates (cord-free, motorized, wired or battery), how it mounts, and how hard the windows are to reach. Your written proposal lists each item so you can see where the cost comes from and adjust options.

What happens at the consultation?

We look at the rooms you're thinking about, show samples at your windows, and talk through how you use each space. If you're ready, we measure. There's no obligation to buy.

Do solar shades give privacy at night?

Often not fully. Many solar fabrics let you see out during the day, but when lights are on inside after dark, people outside may be able to see in. If nighttime privacy matters, we'll show options like tighter fabrics or a second layer.

Is "blackout" the same as completely dark?

No. A blackout fabric blocks light through the material, but light can still come in around the edges depending on how the shade is mounted. If you need a very dark room, we'll talk about mounting, side coverage, and layering.

Do you have cordless options for homes with kids or pets?

Yes, many products come in cord-free or motorized versions. Safety groups recommend cordless window coverings in homes where young children live or visit, and we can point those options out during your visit.
